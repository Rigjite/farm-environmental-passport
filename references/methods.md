# Analytical methods by matrix and the minimal sampling plan

The skill names the method; a certified laboratory measures. A result produced by a method that is not validated for its matrix is printed as **grey** ("method not valid for matrix") until re-analysed.

## 1. PFAS — method by matrix (US)

| Matrix | Method | Why | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Drinking water, incl. a private well used for drinking, when the question is comparison with drinking-water standards | **EPA 533** or **EPA 537.1** | the Safe Drinking Water Act methods; validated for finished drinking water; MCL compliance uses them; 533 adds short-chain PFAS | method scope as summarised by ALS (2024) and others; use a laboratory certified for the method and matrix (in New York, through the State Health Department's laboratory approval programme) |
| Groundwater for a hydrogeological question, surface water, wastewater, leachate, soil, sediment, biosolids, fish/animal tissue | **EPA 1633** (final 2024; 40 PFAS, isotope dilution) | validated for non-drinking-water matrices; not a replacement for 533/537.1 | ALS EnviroMail 2024; BV and Pace summaries |
| Milk and other food; feed (silage, corn snaplage) | **FDA C-010.03** (30 PFAS; single-laboratory validated in, among others, chocolate milk, eggs, silage, corn snaplage) or an ISO/IEC 17025 method meeting EU Implementing Regulation 2022/1428 criteria | food and feed matrices are outside 1633 | FDA method C-010.03 (posted 12 Apr 2024) |
| Human serum | not a passport sample | personal medical decision | — |

Correction against v0.1: the v0.1 sampling plan named "EPA 1633 or the current analogue" for all matrices, including well water. For a drinking-water comparison the method is 533/537.1.

Reporting limits must be low enough for the reference: for well water compared with a 4 ng/L reference the PFOA and PFOS reporting limit should be ≤ 2 ng/L (the level tied to reduced compliance monitoring under the 2024 rule, as stated in state guidance, e.g. Utah DEQ); for EU 'Sum of PFAS' the LOQ should be ≤ 30 ng/L for the sum (Commission guidelines C/2024/4910). The full EFSA four (PFOS, PFOA, PFNA, PFHxS) are requested in every PFAS sample.

## 2. Other parameters

| Parameter | Matrix | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Nitrate, nitrite | well water | report the unit basis (as N or as NO₃); nitrite once; seasonal series (at least quarterly for a year) |
| E. coli, enterococci | stream, pond | seasonal series for a geometric mean or percentile; a single event sample is an indicator, not a classification |
| Total phosphorus, chlorophyll-a, dissolved-oxygen profile | pond | monthly May–September; oxygen profile before any decision on fish or aeration |
| Soil P (Mehlich-3), pH, organic matter | fields | once per three years; depth 0–20 cm (US practice in the examples) or 25 cm composite of 25 cores per ≤ 5 ha (EU 86/278 Annex II C) |
| Heavy metals (Cd, Cu, Ni, Pb, Zn, Hg) | soil and sludge where sludge is applied | EU: strong-acid digestion, atomic absorption reference method, LOD ≤ 10 % of the limit (86/278 Annex II C) |
| N, P (total) | each biosolids delivery | balances use gross N; plant-available N is a separate, stated estimate |

## 3. Minimal sampling plan

Order by value of information: first the sample that can change a status or a recommendation.

1. Re-analyse existing extracts or archived samples for the missing EFSA analytes — no new field trip.
2. Drinking well: the full EFSA four by 533/537.1, quarterly for one year; nitrate in the same visit.
3. The product: tank milk by an FDA-type food method, repeated where model and measurement disagree.
4. Feed from the field with the longest application history and from a control field.
5. Soil of application fields and a control field, with depth stated.
6. Standing water: seasonal TP, chlorophyll-a and an oxygen profile.

Each line in the plan names: object, point, depth or volume, season, analytes, method, and the gap or status it is expected to close.
